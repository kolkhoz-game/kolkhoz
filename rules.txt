KOLKHOZ — CANONICAL PHYSICAL RULES

Players: 3–4
Length: about 30 minutes
Rounds: 5 years
Field quota: 40 hours with 4 players; 32 hours with 3 players

OBJECTIVE

After Year 5, score every card remaining in your face-up Plot and face-down
Cellar. Printed rank is both work value and final score:

Ace 1 · Jack 11 · Queen 12 · King 13 · Saboteur 0

Highest score wins. If tied, most cards wins. If score and card count are both
tied, share the victory. Medals do not score.

COMPONENTS

- 52 crop cards: Wheat, Sunflower, Potato, and Beet, Ace through King
- 1 rank-0, all-crop Saboteur
- 4 Medal cards
- 1 double-sided Central Planner reference card

SETUP

1. For each crop, combine its Ace–4 with one random hidden card of that crop
   ranked 5–King. Shuffle these five cards into a face-down reward pile.
2. Shuffle all remaining crop cards with the Saboteur into the Workers Deck.
3. Give every player a face-up Plot and a face-down Cellar. Only the Cellar’s
   owner may inspect it.
4. Reserve a public area called the North. Cards sent there remain face-up and
   out of play.
5. Place the four Medals in a shared supply.
6. Choose a first dealer and give them the Central Planner card.

Three players use the complete deck unchanged.

ONE YEAR

1. Deal.
2. Reveal one reward for each crop.
3. Determine trump and resolve optional swaps.
4. Play tricks. After each trick, assign every card and check field quotas.
5. Bank each player’s remaining hand card in their Cellar.
6. Resolve requisition.
7. Clean up and pass the Planner left.

PLANNING

Years 1–4: deal 5 cards to each player, reveal 1 reward per crop, and have the
Planner choose trump.

Years 2–5: each player may swap 1 hand card with 1 card in their own Plot or
Cellar before the first trick. The replacement preserves the zone’s visibility:
a hand card placed in the Plot stays face-up; a hand card placed in the Cellar
stays face-down. A card taken from either zone joins the hand and may be played.

TRICKS

The player left of the Planner leads the first trick. Follow the led crop if
able; otherwise play any card. Highest trump wins. If no trump is played, highest
card of the led crop wins.

The winner becomes Brigadier, takes 1 Medal, assigns the trick, and leads next.
Play 4 tricks in Years 1–4 and 3 tricks in Year 5.

ASSIGNMENT

The Brigadier assigns every card from the trick. Any card may go to any field
whose crop appeared anywhere in that trick. Cards from one trick may be split
among multiple legal fields. A card does not have to go to its own crop.

Previously completed fields remain legal assignment targets.

COMPLETING FIELDS

Add printed ranks assigned to each field. At 40 hours with 4 players or 32 hours
with 3 players, the field completes. The Brigadier whose assignment crosses the
quota immediately claims the visible reward face-up into their Plot.

Each field pays at most 1 reward per year. It remains open for later assignment.

THE SABOTEUR

The Saboteur is rank 0 and counts as every crop.

- It is always able to follow the led crop.
- It is a trump in every suit: it beats nontrumps, but every ordinary trump
  beats it.
- If led, there is no ordinary lead crop and every player may play any card.
- Its trick makes all 4 fields legal assignment targets.
- It contributes 0 hours.
- Any field containing it fails requisition, even if that field reached quota
  and already paid its reward.

BANKING

After the last trick, place the 1 card remaining in each hand face-down in that
player’s Cellar.

REQUISITION

1. A field fails if it is below quota or contains the Saboteur.
2. Let N equal the number of failed fields.
3. Every player holding 1 or more Medals is inspected.
4. Resolve each inspected player separately. Pool every card in that player’s
   Plot and Cellar whose crop matches any failed field. Claimed rewards are
   eligible. The Saboteur matches every failed crop and is eligible at rank 0.
5. Send the player’s N highest eligible cards North. If they have fewer than N,
   send all eligible cards. Multiple confiscated cards may share one crop. The
   owner chooses between equal-ranked cards tied at the cutoff.

Hero of Labor: if a player won every trick that year, the Hero is exempt and
every other player is inspected, including players with no Medals. Each other
player still sends up to N highest eligible cards. Exception: if the Saboteur
is in a field, the Hero sends their single highest Plot or Cellar card matching
that field's crop North. If the Hero has no card of that crop, they remain
fully exempt.

CLEANUP — YEARS 1–4

- Return all Medals to the supply.
- Send all unclaimed visible rewards North.
- Reshuffle all assigned and undealt Workers, including the Saboteur if still
  available.
- Do not reshuffle cards in Plots, Cellars, or the North.
- Pass the Central Planner left.

YEAR 5 — FAMINE

1. Flip the Planner to the Year 5 side.
2. Deal 4 cards to each player.
3. Reveal 1 reward for each crop.
4. Reveal the top remaining Worker and immediately send it North.
5. That Worker’s crop is trump. If it is the Saboteur, there is no trump.
6. Resolve the normal optional swap.
7. Play 3 tricks, bank the final card, resolve requisition, and score.

DIGITAL NOTE

The current app has 4 seats and therefore always uses the 40-hour quota. Its
base rules engine implements the same default rules and edge cases above.
